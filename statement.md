# Problem Statement: VIT Mess Wastage & Budget Optimizer

## Problem Context
Hostel messes prepare massive meal volumes daily without accurate attendance data, leading to severe food wastage and financial loss for mess operators.

## Solution Approach
A pure Python CLI software that tracks prepared vs. wasted food, computes financial losses in INR, and utilizes a 3-day moving average along with student attendance RSVPs to predict ideal future preparation volumes.
